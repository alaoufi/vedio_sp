My Video Library — User Guide

A private, offline-first library for downloading and watching videos from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Snapchat. Nothing leaves your device.

1. Activation

On first launch the app asks for an activation code. Your device shows a device number; send it to the developer and enter the code you receive. Activation is fully offline — no account and no internet are required after that.

2. Privacy & app lock

Open Settings to protect the app:

  • App lock: set a PIN so the app asks to unlock on every open.
  • Fingerprint / biometric: unlock with your fingerprint instead of the PIN.
  • Block screenshots: stops screenshots and screen recording inside the app.
  • Hide from recents: hides the app preview in the recent-apps switcher.

Private videos: lock any clip to move it into the hidden private section. It disappears from the main library and only returns after a fingerprint check.

3. Downloading videos

From a link

Copy a video link from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Snapchat, then tap the + button and choose Download from link — or share the link directly to this app from the other app's share menu.

By search

Tap the search icon and type keywords to search YouTube (and TikTok). Tap a result to preview it, or use its download button.

Video or audio

When you download, you can choose the full video or audio only. Audio downloads are saved as real audio files and play with cover art, not a black screen.

Instagram and Snapchat only expose public posts. Private or login-only posts cannot be extracted.

4. The library

  • Grid / list view: switch layout from the ⋮ menu → Toggle view.
  • Sort: by date, name, duration, size, or category.
  • Filter: by source, media type, and multiple categories at once — tick several categories to show only those and hide the rest.
  • Favorites: mark clips as favorites and filter to them.
  • Categories: organise clips into categories; each clip shows its category as a chip. Select several clips to set their category in bulk.

5. The player

  • Continuous playback: when a clip ends the next one in the current list plays automatically. Use Next / Previous from the ⋮ menu.
  • Resume: playback continues from where you left off.
  • Gestures: swipe up/down on the left half for brightness, on the right half for volume.
  • Speed: tap the speed button to cycle 0.5×–2×.
  • Lock controls: hide the controls to avoid accidental taps.
  • Picture-in-Picture & background play: keep watching or listening while using other apps.
  • Resize: fit, zoom or stretch the video.
  • Cover floating text: draw a box over on-screen text or a watermark to hide it during playback.

6. Ad-free YouTube tab

From the ⋮ menu open the YouTube tab: a clean viewer with no ads. Trending is shown for the Arabic region by default, Shorts can be hidden with one switch, and you can search and toggle grid/list. It is a separate tab and never replaces your library.

7. Image editor

For saved images you can crop, hide a name/watermark with a solid box, add text anywhere, and extract text from the image with OCR (copied to the clipboard). Photos are saved cleanly without re-encoding artefacts.

8. Editing clips

From a clip's ⋮ menu you can rename it, edit its description, set its category, add it to favorites, move it to the private section, or delete it.

9. Backup & restore

In Settings, export an encrypted backup of your library protected by a password, and restore it later with the same password. Keep the password safe — without it the backup cannot be opened.

10. Settings

  • Language: choose the app language.
  • Theme: system, light or dark.
  • Wi-Fi only: download only on Wi-Fi.
  • Simultaneous downloads: how many download at once.
  • Save location: where downloads are stored.

That's everything. Enjoy your private video library.
