My Video Library — Full User Guide

A private, offline-first library for downloading, organising and watching your videos. Your library, downloads, database and licence all live on your device — nothing is uploaded to any server.

1. Activation & free trial

The app works offline — no account, no internet sign-in.

  • Free trial: the first launch starts a 10-day free trial automatically. You can use every feature during it.
  • After the trial: the app asks for an activation code. Your downloads and settings are kept — only access is paused until you renew.
  • Activating: the activation screen shows your device number. Send it to the seller and enter the code you receive, then tap Activate.
  • Status: open Settings → Subscription to see “Trial · N days left”, “Active · N days left”, or permanent.
On Google Play the app can activate automatically the moment your subscription is active — no code needed.

2. Privacy & app lock

Everything is under Settings → Security:

  • App lock: set a PIN so the app asks to unlock on every open.
  • Fingerprint / biometric: unlock with your fingerprint instead of typing the PIN.
  • Block screenshots: stops screenshots and screen recording inside the app.
  • Hide from recents: hides the app’s preview in the recent-apps switcher.

Private videos

Lock any clip (from its ⋮ menu) to move it into the hidden private section. It disappears from the main library and only returns after a fingerprint check (open the private view from the ⋮ menu).

3. Downloading videos

From a link

  1. Copy a video link.
  2. Tap the + button → Download from link (or share the link to this app from another app).
  3. Tap Paste, then Get info.
  4. Choose full video, video only, or audio only, then download.

By search

Tap the search icon, type keywords, tap a result to preview it, or use its download button.

Audio vs video

Audio downloads are saved as real audio files and play with cover art (not a black screen). You can also pick a quality when the source offers several.

Where files go

Downloads are stored privately in the app. In Settings → Save location you can also have a copy placed in a folder of your choice so it appears in your gallery/file manager.

4. The built-in browser

Open + → Browser for a general web browser that detects downloadable media on any page.

  1. Type a site or search in the address bar.
  2. Play or open the video on the page.
  3. When media is detected a Download (N) button appears — tap it, pick the item, and it downloads to your library.
The browser keeps scanning the page for a few seconds, so if the button doesn’t appear immediately, start the video and wait a moment.

5. Managing downloads

Open + → Downloads (or tap the download banner on the library) to see active and finished jobs. You can pause, resume and retry. Paused or interrupted downloads resume from where they stopped, and a failed download automatically refreshes its link and retries.

6. The library

  • Grid / list view: switch from ⋮ → Toggle view.
  • Search: tap the magnifier; it filters the library live.
  • Sort: by date, name, duration, size, or category (groups clips by category).
  • Filter: by source, media type, and several categories at once — tick the categories to show and the rest are hidden. The Category entry shows how many are active.
  • Favorites: mark clips as favorites and filter to them.
  • Categories: each clip shows its category as a chip. Manage categories from ⋮ → Manage categories.
  • Select many: long-press a clip to start selection, then set a category, favorite, or delete in bulk.

7. The player

  • Continuous playback: when a clip ends, the next one in the current list plays automatically.
  • Next / Previous: from the player’s ⋮ menu.
  • Resume: playback continues from where you left off.
  • Gestures: swipe up/down on the left half for brightness, on the right half for volume.
  • Speed: tap the speed button to cycle 0.5×–2×.
  • Lock controls: hide the controls to avoid accidental taps.
  • Resize: fit, zoom or stretch the video.
  • Picture-in-Picture & background play: keep watching or listening while using other apps.
  • Cover floating text: draw a box over on-screen text or a watermark to hide it while playing (⋮ → Cover text).

8. Editing clips

From a clip’s ⋮ menu you can rename it, edit its description, set its category, add it to favorites, move it to the private section, or delete it (optionally with its file).

9. Image editor

For saved images you can crop, hide a name or watermark with a solid box, add text anywhere, and extract text from the image with OCR (copied to the clipboard). Images are saved cleanly without re-encoding artefacts.

10. Ad-free YouTube tab

From ⋮ → YouTube open a clean viewer with no ads:

  • Trending is shown for the Arabic region by default.
  • One switch hides Shorts.
  • Search with the magnifier and toggle grid/list.

It is a separate tab and never replaces your library. Return with the back button or ⋮ → Library.

11. Statistics

Open ⋮ → Statistics for a dashboard: total videos, total size, total length and total plays, plus your most-played and recently-played clips and counts by category and by source.

12. Language, theme & settings

  • Language: Settings → Language — choose from 14 languages; the app switches instantly.
  • Theme: system, light or dark.
  • Wi-Fi only: download only on Wi-Fi.
  • Simultaneous downloads: how many download at once.
  • Save location: where a copy of downloads is stored.
  • User guide: this guide always opens in the app’s current language.

13. Backup & restore

In Settings, export an encrypted backup of your library protected by a password, and restore it later with the same password. Keep the password safe — without it the backup cannot be opened.

14. Troubleshooting & FAQ

A download failed

Links from some sites expire quickly. Open Downloads and tap retry — the app fetches a fresh link and continues. Check your connection and that the post is public.

A post won’t download

Instagram and Snapchat only expose public posts; private or login-only posts cannot be extracted. Extraction depends on the platform and can occasionally fail — try again later.

Audio plays as a black screen

Make sure you chose audio only when downloading; audio files show cover art instead of a video frame.

The app locked itself

Your trial or subscription ended. Your downloads are safe — renew (enter a code, or subscribe on Google Play) to continue.

Is anything sent to a server?

No. The app has no backend. It only contacts the video’s own site to fetch what you asked to download.
