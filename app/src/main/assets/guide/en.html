Video Library

Your interactive guide. Everything works offline — your library, downloads and data live only on your device, nothing is uploaded to any server.

Tap any card to open it · drag your finger over it for the 3D depth

01
🔑Activation & free trial⌄

The app works offline — no account, no online sign-in.

  • Free trial: the first launch starts a 10-day free trial automatically; all features are available.
  • After the trial: the app asks for an activation code. Your downloads and settings are kept — access just pauses until you renew.
  • Activation: the activation screen shows your device number. Send it to the seller, enter the code, then tap Activate.
  • Status: open Settings → Subscription to see "Trial · N days left", "Active", or lifetime.
On Google Play the app can activate automatically the moment your subscription becomes active — no code needed.
02
🔒Privacy & app lock⌄

Everything is under Settings → Security:

  • App lock: set a PIN required on every launch.
  • Biometrics: unlock with your fingerprint instead of a PIN.
  • Block screenshots: prevents screenshots and screen recording in the app.
  • Hide from Recents: hides the app preview in the recent-apps switcher.

Private videos

Lock any clip (from its ⋮ menu) to move it to the hidden Private section. It disappears from the main library and only returns after a biometric check.

03
⬇️Downloading videos⌄

From a link

  1. Copy a video link.
  2. Tap + → Download from link (or share the link to this app).
  3. Tap Paste, then Fetch info.
  4. Choose Full video, Video only, or Audio only, then download.

Via search

Tap the search icon, type keywords, and tap a result to preview or use its download button.

Where files are saved

Downloads are stored privately in-app. In Settings → Save location you can also place a copy in a folder that shows up in your gallery / file manager.

04
🌐Built-in browser⌄

Open + → Browser for a general web browser that detects downloadable media on any page.

  1. Type a site or search in the address bar.
  2. Play or open the video on the page.
  3. When media is detected a Download (N) button appears — tap it, pick the item, and it saves to your library.
The browser keeps scanning for a few seconds; if the button doesn't appear at once, play the video and wait a moment.
05
📥Managing downloads⌄

Open + → Downloads (or tap the download banner) to see active and finished jobs. You can pause, resume and retry. Paused or interrupted downloads resume where they stopped, and a failed download refreshes its link automatically and retries.

06
📚The library⌄
  • Grid / list: switch from ⋮ → Toggle view.
  • Search: tap the magnifier; the library filters live.
  • Sort: by date, name, duration, size or category.
  • Filter: by source, media type and several categories at once — an active-filter chip appears atop the library; tap × to return to all.
  • Favorites: mark clips and filter to them.
  • Categories: each clip shows its category as a badge. Manage them from ⋮ → Manage categories.
  • Multi-select: long-press to start selection, then set category/favorite or delete in bulk.
07
▶️The player⌄
  • Continuous play: the next clip plays automatically when one ends.
  • Next / previous: from the player's ⋮ menu.
  • Resume: continues where you left off.
  • Gestures: swipe on the left half for brightness, the right half for volume.
  • Speed: cycle 0.5×–2×.
  • Lock controls / resize: fit, crop or stretch.
  • Picture-in-picture & background play: keep watching while using other apps.
  • Cover floating text: draw a box over on-screen text or a watermark to hide it (⋮ → Cover text).
08
✂️Editing clips⌄

From a clip's ⋮ menu you can rename it, edit its description, set its category, add it to Favorites, move it to Private, or delete it (optionally with its file). You can also trim and compress a clip at high quality to save space.

09
🖼️Image editor⌄

For saved images: crop, hide a name or watermark with a solid box, add text anywhere, and extract text from the image via OCR (copied to the clipboard). Images are saved clean with no re-encoding artifacts.

10
📺Ad-free YouTube tab⌄

From ⋮ → YouTube open a clean, ad-free viewer:

  • Trending content for your region by default.
  • One switch hides Shorts.
  • Search with the magnifier and toggle grid/list.

It's a separate tab and never replaces your library. Go back with Back or ⋮ → Library.

11
📊Statistics⌄

Open ⋮ → Statistics for a dashboard: total videos, total size, total duration, total plays, plus your most-played and recently-played clips and counts by category and by source.

12
⚙️Language, theme & settings⌄
  • Language: Settings → Language — choose from 14 languages; the app switches instantly.
  • Theme: system, light or dark.
  • Wi-Fi only: download over Wi-Fi only.
  • Concurrent downloads: how many download at once.
  • Save location: where a copy of downloads is stored.
  • User guide: always opens in the app's current language.
13
💾Backup & restore⌄

In Settings, export an encrypted backup of your library protected by a password, and restore it later with the same password.

Keep the password safe — without it the backup cannot be opened.
14
❓Troubleshooting & FAQ⌄

A download failed

Links from some sites expire quickly. Open Downloads and tap retry — the app fetches a fresh link and continues. Check your connection and that the post is public.

A post won't download

Instagram and Snapchat allow only public posts; private or login-required posts can't be extracted. Extraction depends on the platform and can occasionally fail — try again later.

Audio plays as a black screen

Make sure you chose Audio only when downloading; audio files show the cover art instead of a video frame.

The app locked itself

Your trial or subscription ended. Your downloads are safe — renew (enter a code, or subscribe via Google Play) to continue.

Is anything sent to a server?

No. The app has no backend server. It only connects to the video's own site to fetch what you asked to download.
